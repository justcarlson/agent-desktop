## hyprland-toplevel-mapping-v1 protocol

The Linux binary contains Wayland client bindings generated from
`hyprland-toplevel-mapping-v1.xml` of
[hyprland-protocols](https://github.com/hyprwm/hyprland-protocols) `v0.8.0`
(commit `044a434ec371f985655389bcef09286b1af3d77c`).
The vendored source and its provenance record are in `crates/linux/protocols/`.
